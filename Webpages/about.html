About me

I am an American living in London, finishing an MSc in Project Management at London South Bank University. Before that I spent eight years in operations across Toytec Lifts, Rhino-Rack USA, Allen Company and Apple: running dealer accounts, managing freight and international logistics, and administering a NetSuite ERP system that I taught myself because the business needed someone to and nobody else was going to.

Most of my work has been the unglamorous kind that keeps a company functioning. Finding out why an order process breaks at the third handoff. Writing the SOP that stops it breaking again. Getting sales, warehouse and finance to agree on what a number actually means. That is the thread running through everything I have done, and it is why revenue operations is where I am heading next.

What I am working toward

A revenue or business operations role in B2B SaaS, where the job is connecting systems, data and teams so revenue actually moves. Right now I am looking for a paid UK placement of 10 to 12 months starting January 2027 as part of my MSc.
